Hey everyone! My name is Renty (also 1337), and I'm developing a game called Voxel Odyssey. This channel will be my development diary. Here I'll be sharing all the progress, new features, and behind-the-scenes stuff from the creation of the game. Voxel Odyssey is heavily inspired by Cube World — the game I’ve loved for many years. Unfortunately, I (like many others) was deeply disappointed by how the original project was handled after its long-awaited release. Cube World had enormous potential, but it was never realized. Back in 2016 I already dreamed of making my own version, but I didn’t have the skills or tools at the time. Now I’ve decided it’s the right moment. I’m developing the game completely alone, investing my own money and time. My father is a professional artist, and I hope he’ll help me with visuals later in development. I see huge potential in this project. I want to create a vibrant voxel world with deep exploration, challenging bosses, interesting dungeons, meaningful dialogues, and a rich story. I’m also inspired by World of Warcraft and Dark Souls. My goal is to make a game where players can: - Create their own servers - Do role-playing - Have long-term replayability I don’t want to repeat the sad story of Cube World. I want Voxel Odyssey to live, grow, and stay interesting for a very long time. If you’re interested in the development process — welcome! I’ll be posting updates here much more frequently than on my other projects. And if you’d like to support the development financially, feel free to write in the comments. Every bit of support helps a lot. Thank you for being here! ❤️
